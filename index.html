Frequently Asked Questions

Useful Things to Know

Have a question that is not answered here? Contact us and tell us about your site or project.

What services can I enquire about?

You can enquire about manned guarding, construction site security, event security, residential security, corporate and commercial security, and mobile patrols. Actual service availability should be confirmed with the company.

Do security officers need an SIA licence?

In the UK, a valid SIA licence is required for individuals undertaking licensable security activities, subject to the applicable rules and exemptions. Requirements depend on the duties being performed.

Which areas do you cover?

London and Coventry are the stated core focus locations. Other locations can be discussed subject to operational availability.

Can I request short-term or longer-term security?

Send the site details, dates, hours and duties you need covered. The company can then discuss feasibility and possible arrangements.

How much does security cost?

Pricing depends on factors such as location, coverage hours, assignment length, risk profile and required duties. A tailored quote requires details about the assignment.

Where is Austra Security Limited registered?

The supplied company details list company number 17041794 and registered office at 128 City Road, London, EC1V 2NX, United Kingdom. Please verify these details against the current Companies House record before publication.

How do I apply for a security role?

Use the Careers link to contact the company. Include your preferred location, relevant experience and any current licences or qualifications required for the role.
